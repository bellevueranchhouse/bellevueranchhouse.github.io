ROOMS & EVERYDAY LIFE

How much is rent?

A regular bedroom is $500 per month. A master bedroom is $750 per month. Utilities and tenant-arranged internet are separate. Compare the room options.

What is included with my room?

Both options include access to the shared kitchen, common areas, and in-home washer and dryer. Regular bedrooms have shared bathroom access. Master bedrooms have a private bathroom and larger closet. Confirm the room’s furnishings during your tour.

Are utilities and internet included?

No. Utilities are split equally among tenants. Internet is usually arranged by the tenants and shared across the house. Ask about recent costs when you check availability.

Can I share a room?

The $500 regular and $750 master options listed here are for single occupancy. If you have a different arrangement in mind, contact us to discuss it before applying.

How do I get to UC Merced?

The drive from 376 Hearst Dr to UC Merced takes about six minutes. Allow extra time for traffic, parking, and walking to class.

CatTracks also serves two stops on M Street, at Bellevue Road and Cardella Road. Fall 2026 weekday timetables show 11–26 minutes from Bellevue Road, or 14–30 minutes from Cardella Road depending on the route and departure. These are differences between listed stop and University Transit Center departure times; add walking and waiting time, and check the current schedule before leaving. See the commute details.

Is my room furnished?

Ask about the specific room you’re interested in, and confirm what’s in it during your tour before you plan furniture or moving day.

What is shared living like?

The house welcomes mixed-gender occupants. Residents are expected to respect roommates, keep shared spaces clean, and keep noise low during quiet hours. Smoking and drugs are not permitted. Guests are your responsibility; overnight stays require prior approval.

Can I bring a pet?

The house has a no-pets policy. Contact the landlord directly if you have questions about your circumstances.

APPLYING & MOVING IN

How do I apply and arrange a tour?

Complete the online application with your preferred room and move-in date. We’ll follow up with current availability and coordinate a viewing around tenant schedules.

What happens after the tour?

If you want to move forward, you and your co-signer complete the screening process through MySmartMove. The next steps are lease review, confirmation of the amounts due, and move-in planning.

How long is the lease?

The house uses a 1-year lease. Confirm the specific start and end dates with the landlord before signing.

What should I budget for at signing?

Ask for an itemized breakdown of the security deposit, rent due at signing, and any screening costs before you commit. Your written lease confirms the amounts and payment schedule.

Is the security deposit refundable?

The security deposit is refundable subject to the terms of your lease and applicable requirements. Document the room’s condition at move-in and move-out, and ask the landlord about the return process.

What if I’m out of town?

Reach out with your dates and situation. The landlord prefers direct communication with the future tenant and an in-person tour when possible.

Who can help with my application?

Email bellevueranchhouse@gmail.com with questions or any changes to an application you already submitted.
